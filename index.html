Pharmacy Manager Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Pharmacy Manager ("the app") is published by Medicheckco. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app works fully offline. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal or business data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

You may enter business records such as supplier names and phone numbers, bills, payments, stock items, expiry batches and cash entries. These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send this data anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and it does not require an account.

Sharing and exports

Data leaves your device only when you choose to share it, for example when you share an order list, export a CSV or PDF, share a supplier statement, or save a backup file. In each case you pick the destination (such as WhatsApp, email or a folder), and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Backups

Backups are files that you save to a location you choose. If you turn on automatic daily backup, the app writes backup files to the folder you selected. The app does not use Android cloud backup.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show your daily reminder about due payments and expiring stock.
  • App lock: if you turn it on, the app asks Android to confirm your screen lock (PIN, pattern or fingerprint). The app never sees or stores your PIN or fingerprint.

Deleting your data

You can delete records inside the app, remove sample data from Settings, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
